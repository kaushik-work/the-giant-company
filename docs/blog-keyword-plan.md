## How every post is built for SEO, AEO and GEO

- **Answer first (AEO/GEO):** the first 40-60 words answer the title's question directly, so search engines and AI assistants can quote it.
- **One primary keyword in H1, title, URL, first paragraph.** Secondary terms and real variants go in H2s naturally. No stuffing.
- **Question headings** (What is…, How do…, Is… worth it?) with a short answer under each, matching how people ask.
- **FAQ block** (3-5 questions) with `FAQPage` structured data. `Article` schema with author (the team), dates and updated date.
- **Comparison tables and numbered steps** where useful: the formats assistants extract best.
- **Real proof:** our actual projects (Moonlight, Trripah CRM, CampusGo, FIDO Exim, Keerthi Builders, FlightKlub) and plain "we tried this" detail. No invented stats. Anything we cite gets a source.
- **Honest stance:** pro custom build, but we say when no-code or off-the-shelf is the right call. That is what makes people trust the rest.
- **Internal links:** each post links to one service page, one solution or compare page, and 2-3 sibling posts. Each cluster has a pillar post.
- **User-searchable:** the blog gets a search box and tag filters (see "Build" below), so visitors can find posts by the same keywords.

## Build

- Blog at `/blog`, post pages at `/blog/[slug]`, tag pages at `/blog/tag/[tag]`.
- Posts live as data files (title, description, primary keyword, tags, body blocks, FAQ, updated date), so the sitemap, schema and search generate automatically.
- **Blog search:** client-side search over title, keywords, tags and summaries, plus tag filter chips.
- JSON-LD: `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage` per post. Add blog to sitemap, `llms.txt` and footer.
- Rollout: 25 posts per batch, starting with all the pillars (★) plus the highest-volume terms. Check Search Console before each batch.

How long a business website takes depends on its size and complexity, and above all on how fast you supply content and decisions. A small brochure site can take a few weeks. A site with custom features, integrations or many pages takes longer. Projects are most often delayed by late content and slow feedback, not by coding.

We avoid quoting a single number because it would mislead. Here is what shapes the timeline.

## Stages of a website project

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Discovery** | Goals, audience, pages, features, competitors |
| **Scope and plan** | Written scope, timeline and cost |
| **Design** | Layouts and look, usually reviewed in rounds |
| **Build** | Development, content entry, integrations |
| **Testing** | Phones and browsers, forms, speed, search basics |
| **Launch** | Go-live, redirects, analytics, Search Console |
| **After launch** | Fixes, monitoring and improvements |

## What makes it longer

- **Content arriving late.** Copy, photos and product data are the biggest cause of delay.
- **Many feedback rounds** or too many people approving.
- **Custom features** such as quote builders, dashboards and payments.
- **Integrations** with CRM, WhatsApp and third-party systems.
- **Migrating an old site** and keeping its rankings. See [website migration without losing rankings](/blog/website-migration-without-losing-rankings).

## How to launch sooner

1. **Write a brief** (below) before you start.
2. **Name one decision-maker.**
3. **Prepare content early,** even rough drafts.
4. **Launch a focused first version** and add pages later.
5. **Agree on feedback rounds** and timelines up front.

## A one-page project brief

- **Goal:** what must the site achieve?
- **Audience:** who is it for?
- **Pages and features:** list them.
- **Examples:** two or three sites you like, and why.
- **Content:** who provides what, and by when?
- **Integrations:** CRM, payments, WhatsApp, email.
- **Deadline and budget range.**

A clear brief shortens the whole project, and see [website development cost in India](/blog/website-development-cost-india) for the cost side.

## FAQ

### How long does a simple business website take?
A few weeks is common for a small site, when content is ready. Complex sites take longer.

### What delays website projects most?
Late content and slow feedback.

### Can I launch in stages?
Yes. Launching a focused first version and adding pages later is often faster and safer.

### Does a new site start ranking immediately?
No. Google needs time to find and process new pages, and it does not guarantee crawling, indexing or position.

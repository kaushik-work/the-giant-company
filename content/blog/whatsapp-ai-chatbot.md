A WhatsApp AI chatbot uses a language model to hold natural conversations inside WhatsApp: understanding free-form messages, answering from your content, asking follow-up questions and collecting leads. It can feel like a quick, helpful assistant, but it needs clear limits on what it may say and a smooth hand-off to a person.

## What it can do well

- **Answer varied questions** about your offer, using your own content.
- **Qualify enquiries** by asking a few questions in a natural order.
- **Understand mixed language.** Many Indian customers type in a mix of English and local languages.
- **Summarise the chat** for your team when it hands off.
- **Work any hour,** which matters on a channel people use late at night.

## Where to keep AI on a leash

| Topic | Better approach |
|---|---|
| Prices and discounts | Scripted or pulled from your system |
| Refunds and complaints | Hand off to a person |
| Legal, medical, financial advice | Do not give; point to a professional |
| Anything it cannot find in your content | Say it does not know, offer a human |

Read [what an AI chatbot should never say](/blog/chatbot-guardrails-what-ai-should-never-say) before you launch.

## Design tips for WhatsApp

1. **Keep replies short.** Chat is not email.
2. **Use buttons** for common choices where available.
3. **Ask one question at a time.**
4. **Confirm what you captured** so customers can correct mistakes.
5. **Make hand-off obvious,** with a stated response time.

## Policy and cost planning

WhatsApp has rules about when you can message customers and how templates work, and messaging costs depend on current pricing. Plan around both, and use an official provider. Add model usage costs on top of platform costs.

## Start with one job

Pick a single job, for example qualifying new enquiries, build it, and read the first real conversations. Expand once it works. ## FAQ

### Can ChatGPT-style AI run on WhatsApp?
Yes. A language model can power a bot on the WhatsApp Business Platform, connected through a provider and your own backend.

### Is an AI chatbot on WhatsApp allowed?
Business bots are allowed on the official platform within WhatsApp's policies. Check the current terms for rules on messaging and on bots.

### Will it understand Hindi, Kannada or Tamil?
Modern models handle many Indian languages, but test with real messages from your customers before relying on it.

### How do I keep it from making mistakes?
Limit it to approved content, add clear rules, hand off when unsure, and review real conversations often.

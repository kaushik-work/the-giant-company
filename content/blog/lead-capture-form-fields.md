A lead capture form should ask only for what your team needs to make the first call: usually name, phone and a short description of what the person wants. Every extra required field gives some visitors a reason to leave, so add a field only when you can name how it changes your first conversation.

## The core fields

| Field | Keep? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | One field is enough |
| Phone or WhatsApp | Usually | The best channel for many Indian buyers |
| Email | Depends | Needed if you send proposals by email |
| What they want | Yes | A short text box or a choice list |
| Company | For B2B | Helps you prioritise |
| Budget | Sometimes | Useful for qualifying, but can put people off |
| Timeline | Sometimes | Helps with prioritisation |

## Questions to ask about each field

1. **Will we use it on the first call?** If not, drop it.
2. **Can we find it another way?** Company website or location can often be looked up.
3. **Will it make people hesitate?** Budget and phone often do. Explain why you ask.

## Required or optional

Make a field required only if you cannot proceed without it. Mark the rest optional or remove them. Use clear labels and helpful placeholders, and validate inputs in a friendly way, for example checking that a phone number has the right length.

## Business examples

- **Service business:** name, phone, what do you need.
- **B2B software:** name, work email, company, what problem you want to solve.
- **Real estate:** name, phone, which project, preferred visit time.
- **Export or trade:** name, company, product, quantity, destination.

## Reduce friction

- Allow WhatsApp as an alternative to the form.
- Explain what happens next ("We will call you within one business day").
- Keep the form above the fold on contact pages.
- Test on a phone first, since most visitors use one.

If a longer form is necessary, consider a [multi-step form](/blog/multi-step-forms-vs-single-page-forms). For B2B, read [whether to block Gmail on lead forms](/blog/should-you-block-gmail-on-lead-forms).

## FAQ

### How many fields should a lead form have?
As few as your team needs for the first call, often three to five.

### Should phone number be required?
If you intend to call, yes. If email is your main channel, you can make it optional. Many Indian buyers prefer being reached on WhatsApp or phone.

### Do shorter forms always convert better?
Usually, but a longer form can produce better-qualified leads. Test with your own audience.

### Should I add a CAPTCHA?
Only if you receive spam. Prefer lighter protections, such as rate limits and server-side validation, which do not annoy real visitors.

The best CRM for a small business is the one your team will actually use, that fits your sales process and that you can afford at your team size in three years. There is no single best product. This guide gives you the criteria, the common options and who each tends to suit, so you can shortlist two or three and test them.

We do not rank products or quote prices here. Features and pricing change often, so check each vendor's current page.

## Criteria that matter

| Criterion | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Fit to your process** | Can you model your stages, quotes and follow-ups without workarounds? |
| **Ease of use** | Will a new salesperson be productive in a day? |
| **WhatsApp and email** | Are chats and emails logged against the lead? |
| **Automation** | Reminders, assignment, templates |
| **Reports** | Leads by source, conversion, revenue |
| **Mobile app** | Does your team sell on the move? |
| **Data export** | Can you leave with your data? |
| **Total cost** | Per user, add-ons and setup over three years |

## Common options, and who they tend to suit

- **Zoho CRM:** a broad suite often chosen by Indian small businesses that want many modules in one family of tools.
- **HubSpot CRM:** known for a generous free tier and marketing tools, with costs rising as you add features.
- **Salesforce:** powerful and configurable, usually suited to larger or more complex teams.
- **Pipedrive:** a visual pipeline tool favoured by small sales teams.
- **LeadSquared and similar India-focused tools:** built around lead management and telecalling-style sales teams.
- **Vertical CRMs:** tools made for one industry such as real estate or travel.

Treat this as a starting list, not a recommendation. Always test with your real data.

## How to test

1. Write down your sales process from enquiry to payment.
2. Pick two or three tools and load 20 real leads into each.
3. Let two salespeople use each for a week.
4. Compare time spent, missed follow-ups and how they felt.

## When a custom CRM is better

If your process has steps none of the tools support, such as building a branded quote from requirements, emailing several partners and parsing their replies, or if per-user pricing keeps growing, a custom CRM can win. ## FAQ

### Which is the best CRM for a small business in India?
It depends on your process, team size and budget. Shortlist two or three, test them with real data and choose the one your team will use.

### Is Zoho or HubSpot better for a small business?
Both are widely used. Zoho is a broad suite, while HubSpot has a free tier and strong marketing tools. Test both against your own process.

### Do small businesses need a CRM?
Once more than one person handles leads, yes. It stops enquiries being lost and shows what works.

### When should I build a custom CRM?
When your workflow has steps no tool supports or per-user fees keep rising.

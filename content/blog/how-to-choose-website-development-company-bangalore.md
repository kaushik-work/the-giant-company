Choose a website development company by looking at work that resembles yours, asking how they plan, build and hand over, and checking that they understand search and lead capture, not just design. Ask who owns the code and accounts, get scope and price in writing, and talk to a past client.

## Start with what you need

Before you contact anyone, write down:

- **The goal.** Leads, bookings, credibility, or sales?
- **The pages and features.** Service pages, a catalogue, a quote form, an admin panel?
- **Who updates content** and how often.
- **Your deadline and rough budget.**

A clear brief gets better proposals and fairer prices. See [what to include in a project brief](/blog/how-long-to-build-business-website).

## What to check

| Area | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| **Portfolio** | Live sites similar to yours, with a brief on the goal and result |
| **Process** | A clear sequence from discovery to launch, with demos along the way |
| **Search basics** | Clean URLs, titles, structured data, fast pages, a sitemap |
| **Lead capture** | Forms, WhatsApp and call buttons placed where visitors decide |
| **Performance** | Good [Core Web Vitals](/blog/core-web-vitals-plain-english) on real devices |
| **Ownership** | You own the code, domain, hosting accounts and content |
| **Support** | Who fixes issues after launch, and how fast |
| **Pricing** | Fixed scope and price, or a clear rate with estimates |

## Questions to ask

1. Can I see two or three live sites you built, and speak to a client?
2. Who will actually work on my project?
3. How do you handle search optimisation from day one?
4. What happens after launch? Who maintains it?
5. Who owns the code and accounts?
6. How do you handle changes during the project?
7. What do you need from me, and when?

## Red flags

- **Guaranteed rankings.** Nobody can guarantee a position on Google, and Google says so itself.
- **No written scope** or a price that changes with every question.
- **Everything is a template** when you need something specific.
- **They hold the accounts.** You should be the owner of hosting, domain and analytics.
- **No plan for lead capture or search.**
- **Pressure to decide today.**

## Local does not mean better

A Bangalore team is convenient for meetings, but good studios work with clients remotely across India and the world. Choose on fit and track record, then use video calls and a shared progress board.

## FAQ

### How do I choose a website development company?
Review similar past work, ask about process, search and ownership, get scope and price in writing, and talk to a past client.

### Should I choose a local company in Bangalore?
Not necessarily. Choose on fit and track record. Good teams work remotely.

### What should a website development company include?
Design, development, basic technical SEO, lead capture, testing, launch support and a plan for maintenance.

### Who should own my website?
You. The domain, hosting, code and content should be in your name.

Flutter is Google's toolkit for building apps. A team writes one codebase in the Dart language and ships it as an Android app and an iOS app. For most business apps, such as booking, tracking, field-work and customer apps, it is a good choice because it keeps cost and maintenance down while giving a smooth, consistent experience.

We built the [Trripah mobile app](/projects/trripah-mobile-app) in Flutter, so this comes from using it, not from a feature list.

## What Flutter gives you

- **One codebase, two platforms.** One team builds and maintains both apps, so features reach Android and iOS together.
- **Consistent design.** Flutter draws its own interface, so screens look and behave the same on both platforms.
- **Fast iteration.** Changes show up on a running app in moments, which speeds up design review.
- **Good fit with a backend.** The app talks to your APIs, CRM, payments and AI features like any other client.

## Where Flutter is a good fit

- Customer apps with accounts, bookings, payments and notifications
- Internal apps for field staff, sales and operations
- Apps that need to ship on Android and iOS at the same time
- A first version that you want to test with real users

## Where native may be better

- Apps built around the newest platform-specific features, such as deep operating-system integrations
- Very heavy graphics or games
- Cases where a team already has strong native Android or iOS developers and one platform only

If you only need to show information, a fast mobile website may be enough. Apps earn their place with offline use, notifications, device features or a faster daily workflow.

## How a Flutter project runs

1. **Discover:** who uses the app and the five things they do most.
2. **Design:** a clickable prototype you can react to.
3. **Build:** short sprints with a working build after each one.
4. **Release:** testing, then publishing to Google Play and the App Store from your developer accounts.
5. **Maintain:** updates for new Android and iOS versions.

## FAQ

### What is Flutter app development?
Building mobile apps with Flutter, Google's UI toolkit. A single codebase produces both an Android and an iOS app.

### Is Flutter good for business apps?
Yes, for most. It suits apps with standard screens, forms, lists, payments and notifications. Very platform-specific features may need extra native code.

### Is Flutter better than React Native?
Both can build good apps. Flutter gives very consistent visuals across platforms. The better choice often depends on your team's skills and the app's needs, so test the shortlist against your requirements.

### Can a Flutter app use my existing backend?
Yes. The app connects through APIs, and we can build or extend the backend alongside it.

### Does Flutter work for iOS and Android both?
Yes, that is its main advantage. You still need developer accounts and testing on both platforms.

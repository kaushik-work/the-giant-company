An AI chatbot suits a travel agency because travel enquiries are conversational and open-ended: dates, group, interests and budget change the whole answer. A good bot talks the visitor into a draft plan, captures their details, and gives your sales team a lead worth calling. It should not quote prices unless your system provides them.

## Where a chatbot helps a travel business

- **Out-of-hours enquiries.** Travellers plan in the evening and at weekends.
- **First-draft itineraries.** The bot turns "7 days in Japan, we love food" into a route the traveller can react to.
- **Qualifying leads.** By the end of the chat you know destination, dates, group and style.
- **Saving agent time** on repeat questions about visas, seasons and basics.

## Where it must be careful

- **Prices.** Costs depend on partners, dates and availability. In Moonlight we made the bot never quote prices, so pricing stays with people.
- **Visas and rules.** Give general guidance and point to official sources, since rules change.
- **Availability.** Do not promise what your system has not confirmed.

## What we built

For Trripah we built Moonlight, which plans trips by chat and hands a ready lead to the team, and we connected it to a CRM that automates the quote steps that follow. In that CRM, one click sends branded quote-request emails to the right destination partners, replies are logged against the trip, and an AI parser turns supplier quotes in email, PDF or DOCX into a structured itinerary that can be shared with the client.

## A practical starting plan

1. Pick one destination type or product to start.
2. Write the persona and rules, including what the bot must not say.
3. Build the knowledge per destination, and load it only when needed.
4. Connect the hand-off to your CRM or WhatsApp.
5. Read the first 50 conversations and improve.

## FAQ

### Can an AI chatbot replace travel agents?
No. It handles the early conversation and capture. Agents still price, confirm and care for the customer.

### Should a travel chatbot show prices?
Only if prices come from a reliable system. Otherwise leave pricing to your team, as we did in Moonlight.

### Can the chatbot create full itineraries?
It can draft them well. Treat the draft as a starting point that your team confirms and prices.

### What does the chatbot need to know?
Your destinations, products and rules, loaded per topic, plus the questions you want it to ask before handing off.

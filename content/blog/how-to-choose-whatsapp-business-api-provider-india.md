Choose a WhatsApp Business API provider on whether it is an official partner, how transparent the pricing is, how good the inbox and automation tools are, how fast support responds and whether you can leave with your data and number. Do not choose on the lowest monthly fee alone, because message charges and limits decide the real cost.

## The checklist

| Criterion | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| **Official access** | Works through the official WhatsApp Business Platform |
| **Pricing transparency** | A clear split between WhatsApp message charges and the provider fee |
| **Onboarding** | Help with business verification and display name approval |
| **Templates** | Fast, supported template submission and approvals |
| **Inbox and roles** | A shared inbox with assignment and user permissions |
| **Automation** | Chatbot builder, auto-replies, routing |
| **Integrations** | CRM, webhooks and API access |
| **Support** | Response times you can rely on |
| **Portability** | Contact and chat export, and a way to keep your number |

## Questions to ask

1. Are you an official provider or partner of WhatsApp?
2. Do you mark up message charges?
3. What does each extra user or feature cost?
4. How long does onboarding take and what documents are needed?
5. What are the limits on message volume and templates?
6. What happens to my number and data if I leave?
7. Can I get references from similar businesses?

## FAQ

### Where can I get the WhatsApp Business API in India?
Through official providers and partners, or via Meta's own cloud offering with a developer. Compare them on the criteria above.

### Do I need a developer to use the API?
For a basic inbox, a provider's tools are enough. For custom flows, AI or CRM integration, you need development.

### Can I switch providers later?
Often yes, but ask first how number migration and data export work.

### How long does onboarding take?
It varies with verification and approvals. Ask your provider for a realistic timeline.

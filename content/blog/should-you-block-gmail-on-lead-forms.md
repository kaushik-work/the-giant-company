Blocking Gmail and other free email domains on a lead form can improve the quality of B2B leads, but it also turns away real buyers such as founders, small shop owners and consultants who use personal email. Block only if your leads are mostly larger companies, and consider softer options first.

## Why teams block free email

- Free-mail addresses are common in spam and test submissions.
- A company email lets you identify the business and research it.
- Larger B2B buyers almost always use company addresses.

## Why it can backfire

- **Small businesses use Gmail.** Many Indian owners, freelancers and early-stage founders run on Gmail. If they are your customers, you are rejecting them.
- **Some people mistype or use shared addresses** and then give up.
- **It adds friction** at the exact moment someone wanted to contact you.
- **It is easy to bypass.** A determined spammer will just use another address.

## Softer alternatives

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| Accept any email, flag free domains | You still get the lead, and sales can prioritise company emails |
| Ask for company name as well | Gives context without blocking |
| Require phone verification instead | Filters junk without excluding small firms |
| Use rate limits and honeypots | Stops bots without affecting people |
| Score leads | Rank, do not reject |

## What we do

On our own contact flow we ask for a work email and reject Gmail addresses, because we wanted enquiries from established businesses and wanted to keep out noise. We know this means a founder who only uses Gmail has to enter another address or reach us through WhatsApp, which is why the contact page also offers WhatsApp and phone. If you serve small businesses or individuals, we would not copy this.

## How to decide

1. Look at your best customers. What email do they use?
2. Count how many past leads came from free domains and how many became customers.
3. If many did, do not block. Flag and score instead.
4. Always provide another way to reach you.

## FAQ

### Should I block Gmail on my lead form?
Only if your customers are mostly larger companies and you have a second way to reach you. Otherwise flag instead of block.

### Does blocking free email reduce spam?
Somewhat, but bots can use other addresses. Rate limits and server-side validation work better.

### What counts as a work email?
An address on a company domain rather than a free provider. It is a rough proxy, not proof.

### What is a good alternative?
Accept any email, ask for company name and score the lead.

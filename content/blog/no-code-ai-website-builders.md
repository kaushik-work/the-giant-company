No-code AI website builders generate a site from a short description, giving you a first draft in minutes. They are useful for prototypes, simple pages and getting past a blank page. They are weaker on accuracy, originality, performance and anything beyond standard layouts, so treat the output as a draft to edit, not a finished business site.

## What they do well

- **Speed to a first version.** A layout, sections and placeholder copy in minutes.
- **Exploring ideas.** Try three directions before you commit.
- **Basic pages** for a small business or event.
- **Lowering the barrier** for people who cannot design.

## Where they usually break

| Issue | What to expect |
|---|---|
| **Generic content** | Plausible but similar to everyone else's, and not based on your real experience |
| **Accuracy** | Invented details, services or claims you must check |
| **SEO structure** | Titles, headings and URLs may be weak or repetitive |
| **Performance** | Extra scripts and heavy pages |
| **Custom features** | Quote flows, logins and integrations are limited |
| **Ownership** | Possible lock-in to the platform |

## Content: the big risk

Google's guidance asks for helpful, original, people-first content and treats mass-produced content made mainly to manipulate rankings as spam. An AI-generated site full of generic copy will not help you stand out. Use AI for structure and drafts, then add what only you can: real projects, real numbers, real photos, your process and your opinions.

## A sensible way to use one

1. **Use it for a draft** of structure and wording.
2. **Rewrite the copy** with your own facts and voice.
3. **Check every claim.**
4. **Review titles, descriptions and headings** for each page.
5. **Test on a phone** and check speed.
6. **Make sure you can export your content** or own the code.

## FAQ

### What is a no-code AI website builder?
A tool that generates a website from a text prompt, with layout, sections and draft copy, without writing code.

### Can an AI-built site rank on Google?
It can, if the content is original, useful and the technical basics are right. Generic, mass-produced content is unlikely to help.

### Do AI website builders give me the code?
It depends on the product. Check whether you can export or own it before committing.

### Is an AI-generated website good enough to launch?
Only after you edit it, add your real information and check every claim.

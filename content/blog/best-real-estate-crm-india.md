The best real estate CRM in India captures enquiries from every source, assigns each to a salesperson quickly, schedules site visits, keeps follow-ups from slipping and shows which marketing source produces buyers. Choose on those jobs, not on feature lists, and test with your own leads before you commit.

## The jobs a real estate CRM must do

1. **Capture leads from everywhere:** property portals, your website, ads, calls and WhatsApp, with the source on each lead.
2. **Assign fast:** by project, location or round-robin, with an instant alert. See [how fast to reply to a lead](/blog/how-fast-reply-to-a-lead).
3. **Schedule site visits** and send reminders to buyers and staff.
4. **Track every follow-up** with notes and next steps.
5. **Show inventory** or link to it: plots, units, availability.
6. **Log WhatsApp and calls** against the lead.
7. **Report by source:** which portals and campaigns bring real buyers, not just enquiries.

## Features worth checking

| Feature | Why it matters in real estate |
|---|---|
| Duplicate detection | The same buyer enquires on several portals |
| Site-visit scheduling | The core conversion step |
| Stage tracking | Enquiry, visit, negotiation, booking |
| Broker and channel-partner access | Separate views and commissions |
| Campaign attribution | Know your cost per booking |
| Mobile app | Sales teams work on site |

## A simple way to choose

1. List your lead sources and where they go today.
2. Write the stages from enquiry to booking.
3. Test two or three tools with real leads for a week.
4. Check export, cost at your team size and mobile use.

## FAQ

### What is the best CRM for real estate in India?
It depends on your size, lead sources and process. Shortlist a few, test with real leads and choose the one your team will use.

### Do real estate agents need a CRM?
Yes, once leads come from several sources and more than one person follows up. Missed follow-ups lose bookings.

### Can a CRM capture leads from property portals?
Many can, through integrations or email parsing. Check the portals you use before you buy.

### Is a custom real estate CRM worth it?
When you have multiple projects, channel partners and logic that tools do not handle, it can be.

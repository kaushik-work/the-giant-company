Website development cost in India depends mainly on scope: the number of pages, the features behind them, the amount of custom design and who writes the content. A simple brochure site and a platform with logins, payments and an admin panel are different projects, so the same word "website" can mean a very wide price range.

We are not publishing a single price here because any universal number would be misleading. Instead, here is what moves it and how to compare quotes.

## The main cost drivers

| Driver | Why it changes the price |
|---|---|
| **Number and type of pages** | A five-page site is smaller than one with a catalogue or hundreds of generated pages |
| **Features** | Forms, payments, user accounts, search, admin panel, integrations |
| **Design** | Template-based vs custom design and illustration |
| **Content** | Who writes the copy, takes photos or produces video |
| **Search setup** | Technical SEO, structured data, migration from an old site |
| **Integrations** | CRM, payment gateway, WhatsApp, email, third-party APIs |
| **Performance and security** | Careful builds take more effort than quick ones |
| **Maintenance** | Hosting, updates, backups and support after launch |

## Typical approaches

- **Template or builder.** Lowest cost and fastest, with limits on customisation. See [no-code website builder: is it enough?](/blog/no-code-website-builder-enough-for-business).
- **Customised CMS site.** A middle path with editing tools built in.
- **Custom-coded site.** More control and speed, higher upfront cost.

## How to compare quotes fairly

1. **Ask each company to quote the same written brief.**
2. **Check what is included:** design, development, content, SEO basics, testing, launch support.
3. **Ask what is not included,** such as stock images, copywriting and extra rounds of changes.
4. **Ask about running costs:** hosting, domain, maintenance and licences.
5. **Check ownership** of the code and accounts.
6. **Beware of a quote far below the rest.** It may leave out things you need.

## Running costs people forget

Domain renewal, hosting, email, backups, security updates, occasional changes and, if you use paid tools, subscriptions. Ask for these in writing.

## Cost vs value

The right question is not "how cheap" but "what will it earn". A site that converts visitors into enquiries pays for itself. Plan for lead capture, speed and search from the start. ## FAQ

### How much does a website cost in India?
It varies widely with scope. Simple sites cost much less than platforms with accounts, payments and custom features. Get quotes against a written brief.

### Why do quotes differ so much?
Different scope, design effort, content, testing and support. Compare what is included, not just the total.

### Are there recurring costs?
Yes: domain, hosting, maintenance and any paid tools. Ask for them upfront.

### Is a cheaper website a bad idea?
Not necessarily. A simple site may be all you need. Just make sure the quote covers what matters to your goals.

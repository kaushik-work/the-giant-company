AI is unlikely to replace CRM. A CRM is a system of record: who the customer is, what was said and what happens next. AI is changing how that record is filled and used: it can read emails and calls, draft replies, summarise conversations and suggest next steps. The record still needs to exist, be accurate and be owned by your team.

## What AI changes

| Area | Before | With AI |
|---|---|---|
| **Data entry** | Salespeople type notes and update fields | AI extracts details from emails, chats and documents for review |
| **Conversation summaries** | Written by hand or skipped | Generated, with a person checking |
| **Lead prioritisation** | Manual rules | Scores and suggestions |
| **Replies and follow-ups** | Written from scratch | Drafted for approval |
| **Reporting questions** | Built by an analyst | Asked in plain language |

## What AI does not replace

- **The system of record.** Someone must own the data, the permissions and the history.
- **Judgment and relationships.** Pricing, exceptions and trust stay with people.
- **Accuracy checks.** AI can be confidently wrong, so important outputs need review.
- **Process design.** A tool cannot decide how your business sells.

## An example from our work

In our Trripah CRM, AI reads supplier quotes in email, PDF or DOCX and returns a structured itinerary. A person confirms it before it reaches a client. The AI removes retyping, while the CRM remains the record and the control point.

## What to do

1. Keep a clean, well-owned CRM as your record.
2. Add AI where it removes repetitive typing and reading.
3. Keep humans in the loop for anything customer-facing or financial.
4. Measure accuracy on your own data.

## FAQ

### Will AI replace CRM software?
Unlikely. AI changes how data is captured and used, but businesses still need a reliable system of record.

### What can AI do in a CRM today?
Extract data from emails and documents, summarise conversations, suggest next steps and draft replies, with review.

### Is AI in a CRM safe?
With limited access, human review and monitoring, yes. Do not give AI more access than it needs.

### Should a small business add AI to its CRM?
Start with one repetitive task, such as summarising calls or parsing documents, and measure the benefit.

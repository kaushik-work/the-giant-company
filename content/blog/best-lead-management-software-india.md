Lead management software captures leads from every source, assigns each to a person, reminds them to follow up and shows what converts. Choose it on those four jobs and on how fast your team can use it. A tool nobody opens is worse than a well-kept spreadsheet.

## The four jobs

1. **Capture:** forms, WhatsApp, ads, portals and calls land in one place with their source.
2. **Assign:** rules by product, region or round-robin, with instant alerts.
3. **Follow up:** reminders, templates and a clear next step on every lead.
4. **Measure:** leads, qualified leads and customers by source, plus response time.

## Features that matter

| Feature | Check |
|---|---|
| Duplicate handling | Does the same person create three leads? |
| Source tracking | Can you see which page or campaign brought each lead? |
| WhatsApp and call logging | Are conversations attached to the lead? |
| Lead scoring | Can you prioritise without complex setup? |
| Mobile app | Can the team work from a phone? |
| Export | Can you take your data out? |

## Questions to ask

1. How long until my team is productive?
2. What happens when two people touch the same lead?
3. What is the full cost at my team size, with add-ons?
4. Can it connect to my website forms and WhatsApp?
5. How do I get my data out?

## A one-week test

Load 20 real leads into two or three shortlisted tools, let two salespeople work them for a week and compare missed follow-ups, time spent and how they felt.

## When to build your own

## FAQ

### What is lead management software?
Software that captures, assigns, tracks and measures leads from first enquiry to customer.

### Is lead management software the same as a CRM?
Lead management is a part of a CRM. Many CRMs include it, and some tools focus only on leads.

### Do I need it if I have only a few leads?
A well-kept sheet can do at first. Once several people handle leads, a tool prevents misses.

### What should it cost?
It depends on team size and features. Compare the total over three years using current vendor pricing.

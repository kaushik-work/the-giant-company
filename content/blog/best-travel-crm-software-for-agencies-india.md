The best travel CRM for an Indian agency handles the parts of travel selling that general CRMs do not: building a quote from a requirement, asking several suppliers for rates, logging their replies, turning them into an itinerary the client can read and collecting payment. Check those steps first; contact management is the easy part.

We run a travel business through the Trripah products, so this comes from building a travel CRM, not only from reading feature lists.

## What a travel CRM needs beyond a normal CRM

| Need | Why it matters in travel |
|---|---|
| **Enquiry capture** | Leads come from the website, WhatsApp, calls and chatbots |
| **Requirement to quote** | Destination, dates, group size, hotels, transfers, activities |
| **Supplier requests** | Rates come from several destination partners |
| **Reply logging** | Quotes arrive by email, PDF and chat and must attach to the trip |
| **Itinerary builder** | A clear, branded, shareable itinerary |
| **Payments** | Link, status and reminders against each booking |
| **Pipeline and reminders** | Follow-up decides who books |
| **Reports** | Leads and bookings by source and destination |

## The time sinks to remove

Typing the same request to several partners, chasing replies, copying prices from emails and PDFs into a sheet, and rebuilding itineraries for every client. ## Ready-made or custom

Ready-made travel CRMs and general CRMs with travel templates suit many small agencies. If your workflow depends on supplier emails, parsing quotes and branded itineraries, check whether the tool does it natively. If not, custom may pay off. ## How to choose

1. Write the steps from enquiry to payment, including supplier requests.
2. Ask each vendor to show those steps with your data.
3. Check WhatsApp, email, payments and export.
4. Compare cost over three years.

## FAQ

### What is the best CRM for the travel industry?
The one that handles quotes, supplier requests, itineraries and payments for your process. Test shortlisted tools on those steps.

### Is there a free travel CRM?
Some general CRMs have free plans, but they rarely cover quotes, supplier emails and itineraries. See the limits of free CRMs.

### Can a CRM talk to our suppliers?
A custom CRM can send and log supplier emails and parse replies. Check whether a ready-made tool supports it.

### Can AI help travel agents?
Yes, for trip-planning chat, reading supplier quotes and summarising conversations, with a person reviewing results.

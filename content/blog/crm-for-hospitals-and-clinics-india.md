A CRM for a hospital or clinic manages the relationship side of care: enquiries, appointment requests, reminders, follow-ups and communication. It is not a clinical record system. Used well, it reduces missed appointments and lost enquiries and shows where patients come from. Privacy and consent matter more here than in most industries.

## CRM vs hospital management software

| | Healthcare CRM | Hospital management software |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Enquiries, appointments, follow-up, communication | Clinical records, billing, wards, lab |
| Users | Front desk, marketing, patient relations | Doctors, nurses, billing |
| Typical data | Contact details, source, preferences | Medical records |

Many providers use both and connect them carefully.

## What a healthcare CRM should do

1. **Capture enquiries** from the website, phone, WhatsApp and campaigns with their source.
2. **Manage appointments** with reminders to reduce no-shows.
3. **Follow up** after visits and for recurring care.
4. **Segment communication** carefully, with consent.
5. **Track the patient enquiry journey** from first contact to visit.
6. **Report** on sources, conversion and no-show rates.

## Privacy and consent

Health information is sensitive. Check where data is stored, who can access it, how consent is recorded and how data can be exported or deleted. Keep clinical details out of general chat and marketing tools, and take legal advice on your obligations.

## Ready-made or custom?

Many clinics are well served by a ready-made healthcare CRM. Hospitals with several departments, unusual workflows or specific integrations may need custom work. See our [CRM development](/services/crm-development) service and [healthcare CRM page](/industries/healthcare/crm-development).

## How to choose

- Map your enquiry-to-visit process first.
- Check WhatsApp and appointment reminder support.
- Ask about data location, access control and export.
- Test with real, non-sensitive sample data.

## FAQ

### What is a CRM in a hospital?
Software that manages enquiries, appointments, reminders and communication with patients, separate from clinical records.

### Is a CRM the same as hospital management software?
No. A CRM handles relationships and communication. Hospital management software handles clinical and operational records.

### Can a CRM send appointment reminders on WhatsApp?
Many can, through the WhatsApp Business Platform, with consent and approved templates.

### Is patient data safe in a CRM?
It depends on the product and set-up. Check access control, data location and consent handling, and get legal advice.

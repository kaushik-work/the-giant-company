SEO gets your pages ranked in search results. AEO shapes content so it can be used as a direct answer, such as a featured snippet or voice answer. GEO makes your content clear and trustworthy enough that AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews cite it. They share most of their foundations, so you do not need three separate strategies.

## The three terms

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full name | Search engine optimization | Answer engine optimization | Generative engine optimization |
| Goal | Rank and earn clicks | Be the direct answer | Be cited in AI-generated answers |
| Where it shows | Search result pages | Featured snippets, voice, answer boxes | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews |
| Key inputs | Content, links, technical health | Short, direct answers and structure | Clear facts, brand signals, crawler access |

## What they have in common

Useful content that answers a real question, a site search engines can crawl and understand, clear information about who you are, and signals that others trust you. Google's guidance for AI features is the same as for search: create helpful content for people, and make sure it is indexable. There is no secret markup that guarantees inclusion.

## A practical checklist

1. **Make pages crawlable and fast.** Fix indexing, redirects, sitemaps and Core Web Vitals. See [how Google Search works](/blog/how-google-search-works-crawling-indexing-serving).
2. **Answer the question first.** Put a direct two-sentence answer at the top, then the detail.
3. **Use clear headings and FAQs.** Phrase headings as the questions people ask and keep answers self-contained.
4. **Add structured data.** Organization, Service, Article and FAQ schema that matches visible content.
5. **Be consistent about your entity.** Same business name, address, services and author details everywhere.
6. **Allow AI crawlers you want.** Check robots.txt for crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. An llms.txt file is optional and Google does not use it, but some AI tools may read it.
7. **State facts you can stand behind.** Dates, numbers with sources, and honest limits are more quotable than hype.
8. **Measure.** Track Search Console data and regularly test the questions your buyers ask AI tools. Our [Search Console guide](/blog/google-search-console-setup-guide) is the place to start.

## FAQ

### Is GEO replacing SEO?
No. AI answers draw on content that search systems can already find and trust, so strong SEO supports GEO.

### What is AEO?
Answer engine optimization: writing and structuring content so a search or voice system can lift a short, direct answer from it.

### Do I need an llms.txt file?
It is optional. Google says it does not use it for Search, but a clear llms.txt can help other AI tools find your key pages. It costs little to add.

### Can I rank in ChatGPT or Perplexity?
You can make your content easy to find and cite, but you cannot buy or guarantee a place. Monitor the questions that matter to your business and improve the pages that should answer them.

### How long does GEO take?
It follows the same timeline as SEO: technical fixes can help within weeks, while authority and citations usually build over months.

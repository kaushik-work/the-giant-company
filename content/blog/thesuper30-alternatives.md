## How to choose

1. **Decide what you need.** Technical fixes, content, local SEO, AI-search visibility or all of it. A full-service agency and a specialist are different purchases.
2. **Ask for evidence.** Case studies in your industry, and references you can call. Our guide to [hiring an SEO expert](/blog/do-you-need-an-seo-expert-how-to-hire) lists the questions to ask.
3. **Beware guarantees.** Nobody controls Google or ChatGPT. Walk away from promised rankings.
4. **Keep ownership.** Your Search Console, analytics and website accounts should be in your name, with read-only access for the agency at first. See our [Search Console setup guide](/blog/google-search-console-setup-guide).
5. **Check the contract.** Prefer clear scope and monthly reporting over long lock-ins.
6. **Match size to budget.** Large agencies suit large sites. Smaller businesses often do better with a focused team.

## FAQ

### What is TheSuper30?
A Bangalore-based digital marketing agency that offers AI-led SEO, content, performance marketing, design and messaging services, according to its own website.

### Which is the best alternative to TheSuper30?
There is no single best. Infidigit and Techmagnate suit large sites, OneCity and HikeMyTraffic focus on AI search, and smaller teams suit smaller budgets. Pick by your goal, evidence of past work and budget.

### How much does an SEO agency cost in Bangalore?
One local guide puts professional SEO retainers in competitive categories at roughly ₹40,000 to ₹1,20,000 a month, but prices vary widely with scope and competition. Ask each agency for a written quote.

### What is GEO and do I need it?
Generative engine optimization makes your content easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to understand and cite. It builds on good SEO, so most businesses benefit from doing both.

### Can SEO guarantee first-page rankings?
No. Google's own guidance says no one can guarantee a ranking. Treat any such promise as a red flag.

Buy an off-the-shelf CRM if your sales process is fairly standard and you need to start now. Build a custom CRM when your workflow has steps no tool supports, when per-user pricing becomes painful, or when automation tied to your operations is the main value. Start ready-made, and move to custom when you can name what the tool cannot do.

## Comparison

| | Off-the-shelf | Custom |
|---|---|---|
| Time to start | Days | Weeks to months |
| Fit to your process | You adapt to the tool | Built around your process |
| Automation | Within the tool's features | Anything you can describe |
| Running cost | Subscription, often per user | Hosting and maintenance after the build |
| Ownership | Vendor controls roadmap and pricing | You own the code and data |
| Risk | Vendor changes or limits | Build quality and maintenance rest with you |

## A scoring framework

Give each statement 0 (no) to 2 (strongly yes). Add up.

1. Our sales process has steps that ready-made CRMs do not handle.
2. We repeat manual work between the enquiry and the payment.
3. Per-user pricing will become expensive as we grow.
4. We need automation that depends on our own data, such as partner emails or document parsing.
5. We want to own our data and code.
6. We have the budget and patience for a build.
7. Our team has outgrown spreadsheets and basic tools.

| Score | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| 0 to 4 | Buy a ready-made CRM |
| 5 to 9 | Buy, but plan automation around it, or start a small custom build |
| 10 to 14 | Strong case for custom |

## The hybrid option

You do not have to choose forever. Many businesses keep a ready-made CRM and automate the gaps around it, or build a small custom tool for the one process that matters most.

## Before you decide

- Write down your process from enquiry to payment.
- List the three most painful manual steps.
- Ask a ready-made vendor whether they handle those steps natively.
- Get a written scope and estimate for the custom option.

## FAQ

### When should I build a custom CRM?
When your process includes steps no tool supports, per-user costs are rising or automation tied to your operations is the main goal.

### Is a custom CRM more expensive?
Upfront, yes. Over time it can be cheaper if subscription fees would grow with your team.

### Can I migrate from a ready-made CRM to a custom one?
Yes. Export your data, map your fields and run both in parallel before switching.

### Can a custom CRM integrate with WhatsApp and payments?
Yes. Integrations are one of the main reasons to build custom.

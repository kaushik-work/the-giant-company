A CRM costs more than its monthly price suggests. Ready-made CRMs charge per user per month, with higher tiers for automation, reports and integrations, plus setup, training and add-ons. A custom CRM costs more up front but has no per-user fee. The fair comparison is the total over three years for your team size.

We do not quote a fixed rupee figure because plans and exchange rates change. Check each vendor's current pricing page.

## What you pay for with a ready-made CRM

| Cost | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Per-user subscription** | The headline price, usually monthly or annual |
| **Tier upgrades** | Automation, reports, API access and custom fields are often on higher plans |
| **Add-ons** | WhatsApp, telephony, document generation and extra storage |
| **Implementation** | Setting up fields, pipelines and imports, by you or a partner |
| **Training and admin time** | A person must keep data clean |
| **Integrations** | Connectors may cost extra |

## What you pay for with a custom CRM

| Cost | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Design and development, scoped in writing |
| **Hosting and running** | Servers, email and messaging services, backups |
| **Maintenance** | Fixes, changes and new features |
| **No per-user fee** | Adding people does not raise the bill |

## A simple three-year comparison

1. Count users now and in three years.
2. For a ready-made CRM, multiply the plan price by users by 36 months, then add add-ons, setup and the tier you will really need.
3. For custom, take the build quote, add three years of hosting and maintenance.
4. Compare, and add the cost of workarounds your team does manually today.

The result flips as the team grows or as your process needs steps the tool cannot do.

## FAQ

### How much does CRM software cost in India?
It varies by vendor, plan and team size. Compare the total cost over several years, including add-ons and setup, using current prices.

### Is a custom CRM more expensive?
Up front, yes. Over time it can be cheaper if subscription fees would grow with your team.

### Are there hidden costs in ready-made CRMs?
Often add-ons, higher tiers for features you need, implementation and admin time. Ask for a full quote.

### How do I get a quote for a custom CRM?
Describe your sales process and the steps that waste time. We reply with a written scope and cost.

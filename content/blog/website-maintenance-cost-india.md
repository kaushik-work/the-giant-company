Website maintenance cost depends on the size and complexity of the site, how many changes you expect each month and how fast you need problems fixed. Packages usually bundle monitoring, updates, backups and a set number of change requests. Compare what is included, not just the monthly figure.

We do not print a single price because it would mislead. Here is how to compare.

## What maintenance usually covers

- **Monitoring** of uptime, key pages and forms.
- **Updates and security checks** for the framework, plugins, hosting and certificates.
- **Backups** and the ability to restore them.
- **Small changes**: text, images, new pages.
- **Performance and search health** checks, including [Core Web Vitals](/blog/core-web-vitals-plain-english) and Search Console.
- **Support**: a named contact and response times.

## What drives the price

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Site size and type** | A brochure site needs less than a site with logins, payments or a catalogue |
| **Platform** | Some platforms need frequent plugin and security updates |
| **Change volume** | More monthly changes means more effort |
| **Response time** | Faster guaranteed response costs more |
| **Who built it** | Taking over an unknown site starts with a review |
| **Integrations** | CRM, payments and WhatsApp connections need watching |

## What to check in a package

1. How many change requests are included, and what counts as one?
2. What response time applies to an urgent problem?
3. Are backups included, and have they been tested?
4. Who owns the hosting and domain accounts? It should be you.
5. Is there a monthly report you can understand?
6. What happens if you leave? You should be able to take everything.

## Do you need a monthly plan?

If you rarely change the site and it is on simple, stable hosting, an annual check and on-demand changes may be enough. If the site brings leads or sales, a plan with monitoring pays for itself the first time it catches a broken form.

## FAQ

### What does website maintenance include?
Monitoring, updates and security checks, backups, small changes and regular speed and search checks.

### How much does website maintenance cost per month?
It varies with site type, change volume and response time. Ask for a written quote based on your site.

### Is website maintenance necessary?
For a site that brings in leads or sales, yes. Problems such as broken forms and expired certificates can cost more than the plan.

### Can I cancel maintenance later?
You should be able to, and to take your code, content and accounts with you. Confirm this before signing.

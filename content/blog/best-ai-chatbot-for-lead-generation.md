The best AI chatbot for lead generation is the one that qualifies visitors in a natural conversation, captures their details at the right moment and hands a lead with context to your team. Judge tools on those outcomes, not on how fluent the demo sounds. We do not rank products here, because features and pricing change quickly.

## What good lead-generation chatbots do

| Capability | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Qualification** | Does it ask useful questions in a sensible order? |
| **Right-moment capture** | Does it ask for details after it has helped, not in the first message? |
| **Accuracy** | Does it answer from your content and admit when it does not know? |
| **Hand-off** | Does the lead reach your CRM or inbox with a summary? |
| **Channels** | Website, WhatsApp, in-app |
| **Control** | Can you set the persona, rules and what it must never say? |
| **Cost model** | Per conversation, per seat or flat? |

## Questions that separate tools from demos

1. Can I see real conversations from a live customer?
2. How do I stop it inventing prices or promises?
3. Where does each lead go, and in what format?
4. How do I edit the knowledge it uses?
5. What does it cost at 1,000 and 10,000 conversations?
6. Can I export transcripts and leads?

## FAQ

### Which AI chatbot is best for lead generation?
There is no universal best. Choose on qualification, hand-off, accuracy and cost at your volume, and test with real visitors.

### Can an AI chatbot replace a lead form?
It can complement or replace it for some sites, but keep a simple fallback. Many visitors still prefer a form or WhatsApp.

### How do I stop a chatbot making things up?
Limit it to your content, add clear rules, hand off when unsure and review real conversations.

### Is a free chatbot enough?
For testing, yes. For a real lead-generation flow, check limits on conversations, branding, integrations and data export.

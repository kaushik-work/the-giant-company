To outsource software development to India well, define the outcome first, choose a partner on evidence rather than price, pick an engagement model that fits your uncertainty, agree scope and acceptance in writing and keep regular contact. Most failures come from vague scope and weak communication, not from the location.

## Choose the engagement model

| Model | Best when | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed scope and price** | The requirements are clear and stable | Changes become negotiations |
| **Time and materials** | Scope will evolve | Needs a budget cap and regular review |
| **Dedicated team** | Long-term, continuous work | You must direct the work well |
| **Phased (MVP then growth)** | The idea is unproven | Needs discipline on what is in the first phase |

## How to choose a partner

1. **Look at similar work** you can try: live products and a client you can speak to.
2. **Ask how they work**: discovery, sprints, demos, testing, hand-over.
3. **Check the people**: who will actually work on your project?
4. **Ask about security and data**: access control, secrets, backups.
5. **Check communication**: overlap in working hours, language, response times.
6. **Compare more than price.** A very low quote usually leaves out something you need.

## What to put in writing

- Scope and what is explicitly out of scope.
- Milestones, deliverables and acceptance criteria.
- Payment schedule tied to milestones.
- **Ownership of code, data and accounts** in your name.
- Confidentiality and data-handling terms.
- How change requests are priced and approved.
- What happens at the end: documentation, hand-over and support.

## Communication that works across time zones

A short weekly demo, a shared board, written decisions and one named contact on each side do more than any tool. Agree meeting times that suit both sides.

## Mistakes that cost the most

- Starting without a written scope.
- Choosing on the lowest quote.
- No access to the repository or hosting accounts.
- Skipping testing and security review.
- Assuming AI tools mean no review is needed. See [AI coding do's and don'ts](/blog/ai-coding-dos-and-donts).

## FAQ

### Is it safe to outsource software development to India?
Yes, with a clear scope, written ownership terms, secure access practices and regular reviews, as with any outsourcing.

### Which engagement model is best?
Fixed scope for clear requirements, time and materials when scope evolves, a dedicated team for continuous work and phases for unproven ideas.

### How do I protect my code and data?
Keep repositories and accounts in your name, sign confidentiality terms and limit access to what each person needs.

### How do I manage time zones?
Agree overlapping hours, hold a weekly demo and keep decisions in writing.

Business process automation (BPA) is using software to run whole business processes, such as onboarding, approvals, invoicing or quote handling, with minimal manual effort. It is broader than automating one task. Done well, it reduces errors, speeds up responses and gives you a clear record of who did what.

## BPA, workflow automation and RPA

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Workflow automation** | Moving work between people and systems along defined steps |
| **Business process automation** | Automating an end-to-end process, often across several systems |
| **RPA (robotic process automation)** | Software that imitates a person clicking through an interface, often used when no API exists |

The words overlap, and vendors use them loosely. What matters is the process you want to improve.

## Examples

- **Quote handling:** a requirement creates a record, requests go to suppliers, replies are parsed and a quote is built for review.
- **Onboarding:** a signed form creates accounts, tasks and reminders.
- **Invoice processing:** documents are read, checked and routed for approval.
- **Lead handling:** enquiries are captured, assigned and followed up automatically.
- **Reporting:** weekly numbers assemble and send themselves.

## Benefits

- Fewer retyping errors.
- Faster responses.
- Clear ownership and history.
- Staff time returned to work that needs judgment.

## How to start

1. List your most repetitive, error-prone processes.
2. Pick one with clear steps and a measurable cost.
3. Map the steps and the exceptions.
4. Choose a tool or build custom. See [how to choose workflow automation tools](/blog/workflow-automation-tools-how-to-choose).
5. Measure time saved and mistakes avoided, then expand.

## FAQ

### What is a business process automation service?
A service that designs and builds software to run business processes automatically, such as quotes, onboarding or invoicing.

### What is the difference between BPA and RPA?
BPA automates whole processes, often through APIs. RPA imitates a person using a user interface, often where no API exists.

### Is business process automation only for large companies?
No. Small teams often benefit more because manual work takes a larger share of their time.

### What should I automate first?
A frequent, rule-based, error-prone process with a clear cost.

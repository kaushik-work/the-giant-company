A CRM for a travel agency should follow the real path of a booking: an enquiry arrives, the requirement is captured, supplier rates are requested, a quote and itinerary are built, the client pays, and someone follows up. A general sales CRM stops at "deal won". A travel CRM has to handle the steps in between, because that is where agents lose hours.

We run travel products ourselves, and the [Trripah CRM](/projects/trripah-crm) is built around this workflow. This post describes how the workflow should run, so you can check any CRM system for travel agency use against it.

## The workflow, step by step

1. **Enquiry.** From the website, WhatsApp, calls or a chatbot. Each one becomes a lead with its source.
2. **Requirement.** Destination, dates, group size, budget and preferences, captured once in a structured form.
3. **Supplier rates.** Requests go to the right destination partners, with replies logged against the trip.
4. **Quote and itinerary.** Rates become a clear, branded quote and a day-wise itinerary the client can open on a phone.
5. **Payment.** Advance and balance with a link, status and reminders.
6. **Follow-up and reports.** Who needs a nudge today, and which sources and destinations convert.

## Where time is lost, and how automation helps

| Time sink | What to automate |
|---|---|
| Typing the same request to several suppliers | One click sends branded requests to the right partners |
| Copying prices out of emails and PDFs | An AI parser reads the supplier reply into structured data, for a person to review |
| Rebuilding itineraries for every client | A builder that turns the requirement and rates into a shareable itinerary |
| Chasing payments | Payment links, automatic status and reminders |
| Not knowing what to do next | Reminders and a daily list per agent |

More on the email step in [automating quote emails from your CRM](/blog/automate-quote-emails-from-crm).

## Can Zoho or another general CRM do this?

A general CRM like Zoho can handle leads, pipeline and reminders well, and many small agencies do fine with one plus a few add-ons. The gaps usually appear in supplier requests, quote parsing, itineraries and payments. Test any tool on those steps with your own data, and read [custom CRM vs off-the-shelf](/blog/custom-crm-vs-off-the-shelf-decision) before deciding.

## What to check before you choose

1. Walk through one real booking from enquiry to payment inside the tool.
2. Check WhatsApp, email and payment support.
3. Ask how supplier replies are logged and whether data can be exported.
4. Compare cost over three years, not only the first month.

## FAQ

### What is a CRM for a travel agency?
Software that manages enquiries, quotes, supplier requests, itineraries, payments and follow-up for a travel business, in one place.

### What should a CRM system for a travel agency include?
Lead capture from all channels, structured requirements, supplier request logging, a quote and itinerary builder, payments, reminders and reports.

### Can Zoho CRM work for a travel agency?
Yes for leads and pipeline. Supplier requests, itineraries and payments may need customisation or add-ons. Test them with your own process.

### Is it worth building a custom travel CRM?
When your workflow depends on supplier emails, parsing quotes and branded itineraries, or per-user pricing is becoming costly. Otherwise a ready-made tool may be enough. Our [comparison of travel CRM options](/blog/best-travel-crm-software-for-agencies-india) helps.

### Can AI help a travel agency CRM?
Yes: reading supplier quotes, summarising conversations and planning trips in chat, with a person reviewing the result.

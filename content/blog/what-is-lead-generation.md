Lead generation is the process of attracting people who may want what you sell and capturing their contact details so you can follow up. A lead is a person or company that has shown some interest. Lead generation is not the same as making a sale. It is the step that gives your sales team someone to talk to.

## Lead vs prospect vs customer

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Lead** | Someone who has shown interest and given a way to contact them |
| **Qualified lead** | A lead who fits your ideal customer and seems ready to talk |
| **Customer** | Someone who has bought |

## How lead generation works

1. **Attract.** People find you through search, social media, referrals, ads or walk-ins.
2. **Convert.** On your website, a form, chatbot, phone number or WhatsApp button turns a visitor into a lead.
3. **Qualify.** You decide which leads are worth a call.
4. **Follow up.** Your team contacts them quickly.
5. **Measure.** You track which channels produce customers, not just leads.

## The main channels

- **Search engines (SEO).** People searching for what you offer find your site. Slow to build, steady once it works.
- **Paid ads.** Fast, but you pay for every click.
- **Referrals and word of mouth.** Often the highest quality.
- **Social media and content.** Builds trust over time.
- **Your own website.** Where most channels end up, so it must convert.
- **Messaging.** WhatsApp is a primary channel for many Indian businesses.
- **Events and offline.** Still strong in B2B and real estate.

## What makes a good lead generation setup

- **A clear offer.** Visitors must know what you do and why to contact you.
- **Easy ways to contact you.** Forms, [WhatsApp](/blog/whatsapp-click-to-chat-link), phone and chat.
- **Short forms.** See [lead capture form fields](/blog/lead-capture-form-fields).
- **Fast follow-up.** See [how fast to reply to a lead](/blog/how-fast-reply-to-a-lead).
- **Tracking.** Know where leads come from and which become customers.

## FAQ

### What is lead generation in simple words?
It is attracting people who might buy and getting their contact details so you can talk to them.

### What is the difference between a lead and a customer?
A lead has shown interest. A customer has bought.

### What is the best lead generation channel?
It depends on your business. For many Indian businesses, a mix of search, referrals, WhatsApp and a good website works well.

### How much does lead generation cost?
It varies widely. Referrals and SEO cost time, paid ads cost per click. Track cost per customer, not just cost per lead.

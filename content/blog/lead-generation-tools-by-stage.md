Pick lead generation tools by the stage they serve: attract visitors, capture their details, qualify them, follow up and measure results. Most small businesses need very few tools, and a clean website, a good form or chat, a CRM and basic analytics cover most of it.

## Stage 1: Attract

| Need | Tool type |
|---|---|
| Be found in search | A fast, well-structured website and Google Search Console |
| Know what people search | Keyword research tools |
| Paid reach | Google Ads or social ads |
| Social presence | Native platform tools |

Set up [Google Search Console](/blog/google-search-console-setup-guide) early. It is free and shows how you appear in search.

## Stage 2: Capture

| Need | Tool type |
|---|---|
| Enquiry forms | A short [form](/blog/lead-capture-form-fields) or [multi-step form](/blog/multi-step-forms-vs-single-page-forms) |
| Messaging | [WhatsApp links](/blog/whatsapp-click-to-chat-link) and [chatbots](/blog/ai-chatbot-for-website-guide) |
| Phone | Click-to-call buttons and call tracking |

## Stage 3: Qualify

Scoring rules, a few qualifying questions in the form or chat, and manual review. See [lead qualification without complex software](/blog/lead-qualification-without-complex-software).

## Stage 4: Follow up

A CRM with assignment, reminders and status. See [custom CRM vs off-the-shelf](/blog/custom-crm-vs-off-the-shelf-decision). Add email or WhatsApp automation for confirmations and nudges.

## Stage 5: Measure

- Analytics for traffic and conversions.
- Source tracking on every lead.
- A simple report: leads, qualified leads, customers and cost, by source.

## A starting stack for a small business

1. A fast website with clear calls to action.
2. A short form and a WhatsApp button.
3. Google Search Console and analytics.
4. A CRM or well-organised sheet with owner and status.
5. A weekly review of leads and sources.

## FAQ

### What are lead generation tools?
They are software and methods that help you attract visitors, capture their details, qualify them, follow up and measure results.

### Do I need a CRM for lead generation?
Once several people handle leads, yes. A CRM gives each lead an owner, status and reminders.

### Are free tools enough?
For a small business, often yes: Search Console, analytics, a simple form and a spreadsheet can carry you a long way.

### What should I buy first?
A good website and a way to follow up. Tools do not help if leads are not answered.

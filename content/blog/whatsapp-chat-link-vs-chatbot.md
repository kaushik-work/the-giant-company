A WhatsApp chat link is enough when a few people message you and someone replies quickly. A chatbot is worth it when messages come in at volume, outside working hours, or need to be qualified and routed. Most businesses should start with the link and add a bot only when the pain is real.

## What each one does

A **chat link** (a `wa.me` URL) opens a conversation with your number. That is all it does. A person has to read and answer every message.

A **chatbot** answers automatically, asks questions, saves details and hands off. It runs through the WhatsApp Business Platform, usually via an official provider.

## Side by side

| | Chat link | Chatbot |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Setup plus running costs |
| Setup time | Minutes | Weeks for a good one |
| Answers at night | No | Yes |
| Captures lead details | Only if the customer types them | Yes, in a structured way |
| Routes to the right person | No | Yes |
| Risk | A missed message | A wrong or awkward automated answer |

## Signs a link is enough

- You get a handful of messages a day.
- One or two people reply within minutes.
- Enquiries are simple and varied.

## Signs you need a bot

- Messages arrive faster than people can answer.
- Customers message outside working hours and you lose them.
- Your team asks the same five questions every time.
- You cannot tell where leads came from or who owns them.

## A sensible path

1. **Start with the link**, with a different pre-filled message per page.
2. **Use the Business app features** such as quick replies and away messages.
3. **Move to the API and a bot** when volume and complexity justify it.

## FAQ

### Is a WhatsApp link the same as a chatbot?
No. A link only opens a chat. A chatbot automates the replies and lead capture.

### Can I add a chatbot to my existing number?
Usually through the WhatsApp Business Platform with a provider, and a number used on the API generally cannot also be used in the regular app. Check your provider's rules.

### What does a WhatsApp chatbot cost?
Setup plus ongoing platform and provider costs, with messaging charges that depend on current rules. We scope this in writing before building.

### Will a bot make customers angry?
Only if there is no easy way to reach a person. Always offer a human.

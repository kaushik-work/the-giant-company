A WhatsApp CRM puts your WhatsApp conversations inside a lead pipeline, so every chat has an owner, a status and a history. Many products sold under that name are really broadcast tools. Before you buy, check that it uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform, supports a shared team inbox and tracks leads through stages.

## What a real WhatsApp CRM does

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Official Business Platform access** | Reliability and compliance, instead of workarounds that risk your number |
| **Shared team inbox** | Several people answer from one number with assignment |
| **Lead pipeline** | Stages, owners, notes and reminders |
| **Automation** | Auto-replies, routing, follow-ups and a chatbot |
| **CRM sync** | Contacts and chats linked to your main CRM |
| **Reports** | Response time, leads by source, conversion |

## Red flags

- It works by scraping or automating WhatsApp Web, which can get numbers blocked.
- It is mainly a bulk-message sender with no pipeline.
- There is no clear answer about consent and templates.
- You cannot export contacts and chats.
- Pricing hides message charges or per-user costs.

## Questions to ask a vendor

1. Do you use the official WhatsApp Business Platform?
2. How do you handle templates and consent?
3. What exactly is included in the plan, and what does each extra user cost?
4. Can I export my data and keep my number if I leave?
5. Can chats and leads sync with my CRM?

## FAQ

### What is a WhatsApp CRM?
Software that combines WhatsApp conversations with a lead pipeline, so chats have owners, statuses and history.

### Is a WhatsApp CRM the same as a bulk messaging tool?
No. Bulk tools send campaigns. A CRM manages conversations and leads. Many products blur the two.

### Does a WhatsApp CRM need the API?
For multi-user inboxes and automation, yes, through an official provider.

### Is WhatsApp marketing allowed in India?
Within WhatsApp's policies and applicable law: consent, templates and respecting opt-outs. Take advice for your case.

AI can build a working prototype of many apps from a description, often in hours. It cannot, on its own, produce a secure, tested, maintainable production app that fits your business rules. For that you need decisions, review and ownership by people. The honest answer is: AI can build a lot of it, and people still have to be responsible for it.

## What AI does well

- **Scaffolding.** Project setup, screens, forms and standard features.
- **Standard patterns.** Login screens, lists, CRUD pages.
- **Speed to a demo** you can click through.
- **Drafting tests and documentation.**

## What it cannot do alone

| Need | Why AI is not enough |
|---|---|
| **Knowing what to build** | It does not know your customers or process |
| **Business rules** | Pricing, permissions and exceptions must be specified |
| **Security** | Authentication, authorisation and data protection need careful review |
| **Data design** | A weak data model causes pain for years |
| **Edge cases and failures** | Code that works once may fail under real use |
| **Integrations and compliance** | Payments, messaging and regulations need accountability |
| **Maintenance** | Someone must fix and extend it over time |

## A realistic path

1. **Define the problem and the core workflow** in plain language.
2. **Use AI to prototype** and show it to real users.
3. **Decide what to keep,** and what the app really needs.
4. **Have a developer review** the architecture, data model and security.
5. **Rebuild or harden** the core with tests.
6. **Launch small,** monitor, and improve.

## Questions to ask before launch

- Where is the data stored and who can access it?
- How are passwords, payments and personal data handled?
- What happens when something fails?
- Can another developer understand and change it?
- Do we have backups and monitoring?

## FAQ

### Can AI build a complete app without a developer?
It can build a prototype. A production app still needs review, security, testing and maintenance by people.

### How much of an app can AI write?
A large share of routine code. The design, rules and verification remain human work.

### Is an AI-built MVP good enough to launch?
Only after review. Check security, data handling and edge cases first.

### What is the risk of launching an unreviewed AI-built app?
Security flaws, data problems and bugs you cannot diagnose, which can cost more to fix later.
